# Food demo photos

The Food Court demo's dish and cover photos (`food-data.ts`: `FOOD_PHOTOS`) and
its three home banners. All come from Unsplash and are free under the
[Unsplash License](https://unsplash.com/license): commercial use is allowed,
and neither permission nor attribution is required. They are credited here as
a courtesy and so they can be swapped out.

- **Fictional vendors.** Every demo vendor is made up — no real brand, name or
  logo. Each tagline ends with "demo vendor (fictional)". Logos are generated
  monograms (`monogramSvg` in `seed-food.ts`), never a real brand's mark.
- **No brands in the photos.** Each photo shows food only. Photos with a
  visible brand, packaging, signage or readable text were left out (a butter
  pack, soft-drink bottles, a newspaper masthead, latte-art lettering).
- **Delivery.** With Cloudinary keys the seed uploads each photo under the
  vendor owner's folder (`food_branding` for covers, `menu_item` for dishes;
  banners under the first demo vendor's `banner` folder); without keys it
  stores the Unsplash URL (square crop for dishes, 2:1 for covers and banners).
